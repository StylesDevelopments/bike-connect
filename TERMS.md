## 2. What the app does

The app connects to a Shimano Di2 shifting system over Bluetooth. It lets the hood buttons on your Di2 shift levers control your phone. The hood buttons are the hidden buttons on top of the lever hoods. With the app, a tap on a hood button can:

- play or pause media, go to the next or previous track, skip back or forward, change the volume, or mute;
- turn the phone's flashlight on or off, or blink it;
- play a bell sound on the phone's speaker.

The app also shows your gear, your Di2 battery level and your Di2 firmware version. If you turn on Send Hood Presses, the app also tells other apps on your phone about each tap (see section 6).

The app receives information from your bike. It does not change any setting on your bike.

## 3. What you need

- Shimano Di2 shift levers with hood buttons. Not all Di2 levers have them. For example, most mountain bike and time trial Di2 shifters do not.
- A Di2 system with Bluetooth. 12-speed Di2 has Bluetooth built in. 11-speed Di2 needs a Shimano EW-WU101 or EW-WU111 wireless unit.
- Hood buttons set to their default setting in Shimano's E-TUBE PROJECT app.
- An Android phone with Android 9 or later and Bluetooth Low Energy.
- For the flashlight actions: a phone with a flashlight.
- No other app connected to your bike over Bluetooth at the same time, for example Shimano's E-TUBE PROJECT app.
- Location turned on in Android settings when you pair your bike. Some phones find Bluetooth devices only when Location is on, so the app asks on every phone. The app does not read your location.

Please check that your bike and phone match this list before you buy.
