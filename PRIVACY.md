# Bike Connect for Android: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains which personal data **Bike Connect: Ride In Control** for Android ("the app") uses, and what we do with it. It covers the Android app only. Bike Connect for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch has its own privacy policy.

## 1. Who we are

Taylor Styles makes the app and is responsible for the personal data in this policy. Taylor is a sole trader in the United Kingdom and trades as StylesDevelopments ("we", "us").

Email: StylesDevelopments22@gmail.com

## 2. In short

- The app has no internet permission, so it cannot connect to the internet. It sends no data to us.
- The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.
- Data leaves the app in only three ways: Send Hood Presses, Google Play ratings and Android backup. Sections 3 and 4 explain them.
- We receive some personal data from Google Play, and from you when you email us or post on GitHub. Section 5 lists it.

## 3. Data on your phone

The app keeps this data on your phone:

- your hood button actions, your bell sound and your other settings;
- the name that you give your bike;
- your paired bike's Bluetooth address, and the name that the bike sends over Bluetooth;
- for the rating request: the number of rides, and the date and app version of the last request.

When your bike is connected, the app also receives your gear, your Di2 battery level, your Di2 firmware version, the maker's name, the Bluetooth signal strength and your hood button taps. The app uses them on your phone and does not store them.

We cannot see any of this data.

**Location.** When you pair your bike, the app asks you to turn on Location in Android settings, because some phones find Bluetooth devices only when Location is on. The app does not have the location permission, and it does not read your location.

**Android backup.** If Android backup is on, Android can copy the data in the list above to your Google account, so you can restore it on a new phone. Google runs this backup under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can turn off backup in Android settings.

**Delete this data.**

- Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings. This does not delete the copy in Android backup. If backup is on, Android restores the copy when you install the app again. To delete the copy, turn off backup or delete the backup in your Google account.
- To delete your bike's name and Bluetooth address, tap Disconnect.
- Restore Default Settings resets your settings. It keeps your paired bike, the number of rides and the date of the last rating request. To show it, tap Version 7 times in Settings.

## 4. Data that goes to other apps on your phone

- **Send Hood Presses.** This setting is off until you turn it on. When it is on and your bike is connected, the app sends a message to the apps on your phone for each single or double tap. The message gives only the side (left or right) and the tap (single or double). It does not include your bike's name or Bluetooth address. Any app on your phone that listens for this message can receive it. You decide whether to turn it on. The app cannot choose which apps on your phone receive it.
- **Ratings.** Google Play may ask you to rate the app. Google shows this request and handles your rating under the Google Privacy Policy.

**Commands and links.** These carry no personal data. Play, pause, skip and track commands go to the media app that Android chooses. Volume and mute commands go to Android. Help has links to other websites, for example BetterShifting, YouTube and the Bike Connect page on GitHub. These websites have their own privacy policies.

## 6. Who receives your data

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising.

- **Google.** Google runs Google Play and Android backup. Google decides how it uses your purchase, your crash reports, your backup and your rating, under the Google Privacy Policy. Google gives us only the records in section 5. Google also provides our email service, Gmail.
- **Anthropic.** We use Claude, an AI assistant from Anthropic, to read and summarise reviews, crash reports, statistics and GitHub posts.
- **GitHub.** Our private task list is on GitHub. We can copy review text or crash details into it to plan fixes.
- **The developer of Bike Connect for iPhone.** Bike Connect for iPhone and older versions of the Android app show a different email address, watchcloud.app@gmail.com. The developer of Bike Connect for iPhone reads that inbox. If you send an email about the Android app to that address, the developer of Bike Connect for iPhone reads it and can forward it to us.
- **Our accountant**, if we use one, to prepare our tax return.
- **HM Revenue and Customs, a court or another authority**, when the law requires it.

We keep this data in our Google accounts, in our GitHub account and on our own computer. Passwords protect them, and the computer's disk is encrypted.

## 7. Transfers outside the UK

Google, Anthropic and GitHub can process this data outside the United Kingdom, for example in the United States. For Google Play data, Google uses the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement, section 9.3). For the other transfers, we rely on the safeguards that each company offers under UK data protection law. Email us for details of these safeguards.

## 8. How long we keep data

- **Order records:** Google keeps them in Google Play Console for the period that Google sets. We keep our own copies, and emails about refunds or payments, until 5 years after the 31 January tax return deadline for the tax year of the sale, because HM Revenue and Customs requires this.
- **Other emails:** 2 years after the last email between us, unless we need them for a refund or a legal claim.
- **Crash reports and statistics:** Google keeps them in Google Play Console for the period that Google sets.
- **Reviews and GitHub posts:** they stay on Google Play or GitHub until you delete them. We keep copies in our task list and in our chats with Claude for up to 2 years. Anthropic keeps chats for the period in its own terms.

## 9. Your rights

You have the right to:

- get a copy of your personal data;
- correct data that is wrong;
- ask us to delete your data;
- ask us to limit how we use your data;
- object to our use of your data, where we use it for our legitimate interests;
- get your data in a form that you can give to someone else.

To use these rights, email us. We answer within one month. If a request is complex, we can take up to two more months, and we tell you why within the first month. Some rights have limits; for example, we must keep order records for tax.

If you are not happy with how we handle your data, please email us first, so that we can try to help. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at [ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/) or on 0303 123 1113. If you live in the European Union, you can also complain to the data protection authority in your country.
